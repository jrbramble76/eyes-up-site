Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Eyes Up Basketball helps parents, coaches, and basketball fans track individual player statistics while staying focused on the game. We respect your privacy and believe your basketball data should remain under your control.

Information stored on your device

Eyes Up Basketball stores information locally on your device to provide its features, including:

  • Basketball statistics and game events
  • Game dates, timestamps, and season history
  • Bookmarks and game period information
  • Selected statistics and tracking preferences
  • Team colors, themes, and display settings

The app does not require an account or ask for player names, email addresses, birth dates, or other identifying profile information.

How your information is used

Stored information is used to calculate, display, and organize basketball statistics and to remember your app preferences.

Recorded game information is not uploaded to an Eyes Up Basketball application server. The app does not provide cloud synchronization.

Sharing your statistics

You can choose to copy your basketball statistics using the app's Copy feature. This places the selected information on your device's clipboard.

Clipboard information may be accessed by other apps when pasted or synchronized through supported operating-system features. You control where you paste or share your copied statistics.

Analytics and advertising

Eyes Up Basketball does not include developer-integrated advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking services. We do not use your recorded basketball statistics for advertising.

Data storage and deletion

Your game information and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app provides the following controls:

  • Clear game removes recorded events from the current game.
  • Clear season removes archived games and recorded events from the current game.
  • Reset app removes locally stored game information and app preferences.

These actions do not remove statistics previously copied into other apps, clipboard contents, or copies retained through device backups or transfers.

Children's privacy

Eyes Up Basketball can be used to record basketball performance information about youth players. The app does not require children to create accounts or provide personal profile information.

Recorded player statistics are stored locally on the user's device and are not uploaded to an Eyes Up Basketball application server.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted here with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how Eyes Up Basketball handles data, please visit our support page.
